## How it works

Twice a week an AI searches public websites of municipalities, clubs, venues and
regional calendars and summarizes the events briefly in its own words. Every
event links to its source.

**Instagram and Facebook** are not included: neither allows its pages to be read
automatically. Many clubs, cafés and bars announce their events only there. Such
events are missing here unless they are also on a website or a public calendar,
for example the municipality's. [Is your event missing?](../missing-event/)

**Note:** Events are collected automatically by AI and may be wrong or outdated.
Please check with the organizer before you go.
