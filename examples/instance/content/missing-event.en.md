# Why is my event not here?

{{ site_name }} collects events automatically from public websites. This is the
surest way to get your event into the calendar:

1. **Put it on a public website.** The site reads websites of municipalities,
   clubs, venues and regional calendars, but **not** Facebook or Instagram.
2. **Write date, time and place as text**, not only on a poster or flyer image.
   The AI cannot read images reliably.
3. **Publish early**, ideally at least a week before. The search runs twice a
   week, up to four weeks ahead.
4. **A public calendar helps:** also add the event to the event calendar of your
   municipality or district.

Still missing? Send the address of your website to
[{{ contact_email }}](mailto:{{ contact_email }}?subject=Source%20suggestion) or use the
[feedback form](../feedback/). After a short check it is added to the list of
sources that are checked on every run.
